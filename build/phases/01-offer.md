# Phase 01 — The offer & case study

## Context
Everything downstream — templates, pulse framing, qualification, demo pitch — personalises FROM this document. Built first so the machine never aims a fuzzy offer at a sharp lead list. The Property Pulse already carries the positioning line: "Shonichi handles your guest communications 24/7 and turns every message into operational intelligence. Connected to your live guest messages, the insights compound monthly."

## Objective
`core/offer.md` becomes the single source of truth for what Shonichi sells, to whom, at what price, with proof.

## Definition of done
A stranger could read `core/offer.md` and pitch Shonichi accurately, including handling "what does it cost" and "who else uses it".

## Deliverables
- `core/offer.md` filled: product description (guest comms 24/7 + operational intelligence), the offer ladder (free pulse → demo on their own data → paid subscription), pricing, the Altoluxo case study with real numbers, the credibility line (founders run Higgihaus, a real STR operation — peer, not vendor).
- Draft ratified with Sotirios (at the weekly touchpoint if he is not in the session).

## Decisions to make in this session
1. **Price point and packaging.** Reference: Altoluxo pays £493/month for 5-8 hrs/month of service. Recommendation: anchor list pricing near that shape; decide whether there are tiers by unit count.
2. **Does pricing appear in outreach or at demo?** Recommendation: at demo. Outreach sells the pulse and the conversation, not the subscription.
3. **What the demo actually is.** Recommendation: a walkthrough of THEIR pulse plus what the live-connected version would surface — the pulse is the demo's first half by design.

## Inputs required (bring to the session)
- Altoluxo's real numbers: hours saved, response times, review response rate, anything measurable since onboarding.
- Current honest description of what the product does TODAY vs roadmap (sell today's product).

## Session plan (60 min)
1. (10) Dump answers to the inputs above in plain speech; Claude structures.
2. (20) Draft the offer ladder and product description; fight over every claim being true.
3. (15) Write the Altoluxo case study.
4. (10) Pricing decision + where it appears.
5. (5) Log, commit, push. Flag for Sotirios ratification.
