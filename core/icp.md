## The one-minute test

Answer in order. The first "no" ends it.

| # | Gate | Yes looks like | No looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Professional host running it as a business** | Branded host name (a company, not a person), direct booking website, Ltd company at Companies House, LinkedIn company page, professional photography, portfolio review count in the hundreds | Personal-name host, no website, phone-photo listings, "we" is one person. Mom-and-pop tyre kickers are out whatever the unit count |
| 2 | **15 to 150 listings** | Counted on the Airbnb host profile or the direct booking site | Under 15: cannot be billed (minimum 15). Over 150: already has systems, a head of ops and an in-house answer |
| 3 | **Urban serviced accommodation** | City apartments, aparthotel-style blocks, corporate and leisure mix | Coastal or rural holiday-let agencies. Aparthotels on a hotel PMS (Mews, Cloudbeds) |
| 4 | **On Hostaway** | Direct booking site on a holidayfuture.com domain. Hostaway named on socials, in a testimonial or case study. Confirmed on a call | Any other PMS. Do not delete: mark **Parked** (see below) |
| 5 | **Reachable decision maker** | Founder, co-founder, CEO, owner, managing director on LinkedIn | Only a generic company page or a front-desk contact |

Five yeses: **Qualified**. Owner-operators rank above property managers who manage for landlords, because they make financial decisions without asking anyone.

## Parked, not dead

Operators that pass gates 1, 2, 3 and 5 but are not on Hostaway are stored with state Parked. No outreach. They come back when the Hostaway Marketplace listing is live and 2 to 5 clients have proven the model, at which point a Guesty pilot is the first expansion. Elina PMS has offered an integration; later.

## Competing comms tool is not a disqualifier

Conduit (comes up most, usually too expensive), Besty (two operators tried it and stopped), Enso Connect (once). An operator using any of these is a **Operational Intelligence** prospect. The reports read Hostaway messages whatever answered them, and the escalation report measures their VA team regardless. Record the tool in the lead record and route to the OpIntel-only sequence.

## Buy signals, ranked

What actually made an operator lean in on calls:

1. **A recurring complaint pattern across reviews, with the fix attached.** Same access failure, same cleanliness theme, same unit. They have only ever seen these one review at a time.
2. **A declining quarterly rating trend** on a listing or the portfolio. A trend in a dataset, not a bad week.
3. Supporting signals, useful for personalisation, not leads on their own: unanswered negative reviews, response rate under 95% or over an hour, listing rating below the host's portfolio average, no Superhost.

Every first message must reference signal 1 or 2 from the prospect's own pulse. That is the quality-touch rule made concrete.

## Who we talk to

- **Primary: the founder.** Seb sells peer to peer: operator to operator, the same business struggles. Titles: founder, co-founder, CEO, owner, managing director.
- **Back door: the ops manager.** Not every founder is reachable. An ops manager who sees their own team's escalation report becomes the internal champion. Titles: operations manager, head of operations, guest experience manager.

## Geography and density

- Density is the constraint: roughly 7 to 9 professional operators per UK city, 2 to 5 of them large, and only a share on Hostaway. Plan on about 3 hard-qualified operators per city. Ten clients therefore needs 30+ cities worked, not 5.
- **Wave 1 (UK):** Manchester, Liverpool, Bristol, Cardiff, Newport, Brighton. Where top-end operators cluster and where Seb can talk market on the phone. Selling to Higgihaus's own Bristol and Cardiff competitors is fine; Altoluxo already is one.
- **Wave 2 (UK):** Birmingham, Leeds, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Newcastle, Nottingham, Leicester, Sheffield, Bath, Oxford, Cambridge, York, Belfast, Swansea.
- **Wave 3:** London. A different kettle, less forward, less friendly. Only when waves 1 and 2 are exhausted.
- **Wave 4:** US, and any other market. Hostaway is dense there and the pulse works on any Airbnb listing. Peer credibility is thinner and the time zone costs sessions, so it waits until the UK loop has converted.
