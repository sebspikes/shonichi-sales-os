# Phase 02 — ICP & channel map

## Context
A scraper pointed at the wrong ICP sends 500 wrong messages fast. This phase defines exactly who we hunt and ranks where they live, so Phase 06's pipeline has coordinates.

## Objective
Given any operator, `core/icp.md` answers qualified yes/no in under a minute. `core/channels.md` ranks every lead source with an honest expected-quality note.

## Definition of done
Both files filled and agreed. The first target cities chosen for the Airbnb-first pipeline.

## Deliverables
- `core/icp.md`: operator profile — unit count range (working hypothesis: 5-50 listings, professionalised, branded host profile), PMS signals, geography (working hypothesis: UK first), pain signals (unanswered reviews, declining ratings, slow response indicators — the things a pulse surfaces), disqualifiers (giant agencies with in-house teams? single-unit hobbyists? decide).
- `core/channels.md` ranked, at minimum covering:
  1. **Airbnb-first pipeline** (primary; the Phase 06 machine)
  2. **LinkedIn + pulse delivery** (the primary outreach channel the pipeline feeds)
  3. **Fiverr productised gig** — a paid discovery funnel: operators buying STR admin/VA help are buying what Shonichi replaces. Scope the gig here, launch decision at Phase 10 review.
  4. **Hostaway marketplace listing** (inbound; live when Asaad delivers — chase status)
  5. **Intent signals** (job posts hiring guest-comms VAs — priority flags, not a pipeline)
  6. **Directories/watering holes** (STAA membership, PMS partner pages — seeder list)
  7. Anything the founders add from market knowledge.

## Decisions to make in this session
1. Unit-count band and disqualifiers (recommendation above; make it explicit).
2. First 3-5 target cities (recommendation: start where you know the market — Manchester + comparable UK STR cities).
3. Fiverr gig: scope now, build now or park to Phase 10? Recommendation: scope only; the LinkedIn+pulse motion is the September priority.

## Inputs required
Founder market knowledge: which operators/brands you already know, which cities you can speak about credibly.

## Session plan (60 min)
1. (15) ICP: profile, signals, disqualifiers — argue the edges.
2. (10) City list.
3. (20) Channel map: rank, one honest paragraph per channel on expected lead quality and cost.
4. (10) Fiverr gig scope sketch.
5. (5) Log, commit, push.

## Feeds into
Phase 03 (ICP fields become Airtable fields), Phase 06 (cities + channels are the pipeline's inputs), Phase 04 (channel list determines which sequences exist).
