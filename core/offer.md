## One-line pitch

Shonichi handles your guest communications 24/7 and turns every message into operational intelligence. Connected to your live guest messages, the insights compound monthly.

## What Shonichi does today (sell this, nothing else)

Two products, sold separately or together. Both run on Hostaway.

### 1. Guest Comms (the AI layer)
- Every inbound guest message on every OTA channel flowing through Hostaway is read within seconds.
- The AI answers only from a per-listing fact sheet we build for the client: property overview, check-in, check-out, amenities, house rules, parking and transport, local area, known issues, FAQ.
- Response time under 60 seconds, 24/7, every day of the year.
- Around half of all messages are resolved with no human involved (Altoluxo: 47% June 2026, 55% May 2026). The rate rises as the fact sheets mature.
- **What the AI never handles:** anything involving money, bookings, cleanliness, maintenance, pests, damage, safety, lock-outs, complaints, pets, parties, local recommendations, a request to speak to a person, or a photo. These go straight to a human, every time, with full context, routed by shift to WhatsApp, Slack or email. A 498-term pre-filter catches them before the AI sees them, and an output filter blocks any reply that leaks internal references or is a non-answer.
- The client's own team handles escalations. Shonichi never staffs the human layer.

### 2. Operational Intelligence (the reports)
- Every message is logged with topic, sentiment, stay phase (pre-arrival, during stay, post-checkout) and response time. Every review is analysed.
- **Operational Intelligence Report**, monthly, interactive: all messages tagged, complaint narratives, property-level performance, guest incident analysis, high-touch stays, busiest hours and days, recurring issues, priority actions with owner and cost of inaction, month-on-month tracking, an efficiency score out of 100.
- **Escalation Response Intelligence Report**, monthly, interactive: how the human team performs on what the AI escalated. Response rate, median and tail response times, per-agent attribution, coverage by hour and day, escalations by reason, review-risk scoreboard, critical threads, targets for next month.
- Reports are delivered as interactive HTML with drill-down into every incident, property and guest thread. The client reads them once a month with their ops lead and acts on them.

### Why the intelligence is the product
Guest comms automation is commoditised. What no one else gives an operator is a monthly, evidence-based view of where their problems cluster, which units are quietly bleeding reviews, which guests took the most resource, and whether last month's fixes moved the numbers. That is the reason clients stay, and it is priced accordingly.

Serviced accommodation operator, Bristol, Leicester and South Wales. Client since March 2026. 68 listings at onboarding, 54 today. Bundle client. Public reference, happy to take calls. Case study uses aggregate numbers only, never guest or property names.

**June 2026, one month, from the client's own reports:**

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Guest messages processed | 581 across 138 reservations and 44 listings |
| Resolved autonomously | 47% (55% in May) |
| AI response time | under 60 seconds |
| Escalated to the human team | 308, with an estimated 37 VA hours on escalations |
| Human team response rate | 97%, 100% on urgent |
| Human team median response | 21 minutes |
| Complaint stays | 12 (8.7%), down from 14 in May |
| Negative sentiment | 10.5%, down from 15.2% in May |

Time saved: roughly 270 messages a month handled with no human, at 2 to 5 minutes each, is 10 to 20 hours of VA time a month, plus the fact that cover is 24/7 rather than shift-bound. In Joel's words, "a VA and a half".

**What the intelligence surfaced:** a single unit producing five cleanliness complaints across two guests, a recurring during-stay lock-out pattern at four named units traced to hardware rather than messaging, and 19 invoice requests and 9 extension requests a month that were being handled manually.
