# Objection library

> STATUS: STARTED Phase 04 (Seb, 6 Sept 2026). Add every real objection with the reply that worked, forever.

Format: the objection, the truth behind it, the reply in Seb's voice. Sotirios swaps "we run" for "my co-founder Seb runs".

## "We already have a VA for this"
Truth: so does Altoluxo. The AI handles around half the messages so the VA handles what needs a human, and the escalation report shows how the VA is doing.
Reply: "Good, so does our first client. Their VAs still do every lock-out, complaint and refund. What changed is the AI took the 50% that didn't need them, and they now get a monthly report on how fast the team answers what's left. Worth seeing what that looks like on your numbers?"

## "We use Hostaway's own automations / message templates"
Truth: templates answer known questions at fixed triggers. Shonichi answers whatever the guest actually asked, from a fact sheet per listing, and escalates the rest.
Reply: "We run those too. The gap is everything a template can't predict, which is most of what guests type. Ours answers from a fact sheet we build per listing, escalates anything about money, access, cleaning or complaints to your team, and logs every message so you get the monthly picture. The report is the part no template gives you."

## "We use Conduit / Besty / Enso Connect"
Truth: not a disqualifier. Sell the intelligence layer only.
Reply: "Then keep it. We don't need to replace it. What we'd add is the reporting: every message it handles, tagged and turned into a monthly view of where problems cluster and how your team responds when it escalates. Different layer. Want to see the report our first client gets?"

## "How much?"
Truth: pricing is per listing on sliding bands and is given on the call, not in outreach.
Reply: "Per listing, and it slides down as you scale. Depends on whether you want the comms, the reporting, or both. Easier on a call with your numbers in front of us, 20 minutes: [calendly]."

## "Is this AI slop?"
Truth: the reply pulls from their own report, so the answer is evidence.
Reply: "Fair question. The report you've got is built from your own reviews and listing, nothing generic in it. The 399-day access complaint pattern on page 3 wasn't written by a template. The comms side is the same: it answers from a fact sheet we write for each listing, and hands anything it isn't sure of to your team."

## "Where did you get my data?"
Truth: public Airbnb listing and review data only. Nothing scraped from private sources.
Reply: "All public: your Airbnb listing page and reviews, the same things any guest sees. Nothing private. Happy to delete the report if you'd rather, but it's yours to keep either way."

## "We're on Guesty / another PMS"
Truth: Hostaway only today. Park the lead.
Reply: "Then we're not the right fit yet. We're Hostaway only for now. If that ever changes on your side, or ours, I'll let you know. Keep the report."

## "Not now / busy season"
Truth: fine. Set next action for 60 days and go dormant.
Reply: "Understood. The report doesn't expire. I'll check back after the season, and if anything in it is useful before then, you have my number."

## "Send me some info"
Truth: the info is the report they already have. The next step is a call, not a PDF.
Reply: "The report is the info, it's your listing not a brochure. The bit I can't put in a document is what the live version would show for your whole portfolio. That's 20 minutes on a call: [calendly]."

## "We're too small for this" (10 to 20 listings)
Truth: Joel's own words are that under 10 units may not see the value. 15 is the billing floor.
Reply: "At your size the comms saving is real but the reporting is where it earns its place: it's the difference between seeing a bad review and seeing the pattern behind it. If you're growing, this is the moment to have the data from the start."
