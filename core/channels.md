# Channel map

> STATUS: DRAFTED Phase 02 (Seb, 3 Sept 2026). Awaiting Sotirios ratification. LOCKED CORE: changes need both founders.

Ranked by expected quality conversations started per founder-hour. One honest paragraph each. Sotirios brings no existing network; Seb is the operator and leads with his. Every channel below is a human send, never automated.

## 1. LinkedIn + pulse delivery (primary outreach)

The channel the pipeline feeds. Connection request from a founder's personal profile, then a short message referencing one true finding from the prospect's own pulse, with the shonichi.ai report link sent inside the chat. The pulse is hosted on our domain, opens in one click, no email gate. Expected quality: highest of any outbound channel because every message is personal and evidence-led, but volume is capped by the human send rule at about 5 quality touches per founder per session. Cost: Sales Navigator, one seat on Seb's account for search and list-building, decision made this session. Sotirios sends from his own profile off lists Seb builds in Airtable. Status: a few pulses have gone out from Sotirios, volume unknown, to be confirmed at the 12 September onboarding evening. Later: click and dwell tracking on report links, useful once paid or top-of-funnel traffic exists, not needed for v1.

## 2. Airbnb-first sourcing pipeline (the machine behind channel 1)

Scrape Airbnb by city for host profiles that pass the professional-host and unit-count gates, then enrich to a company and a founder on LinkedIn, then generate the pulse. Built in Phase 06, qualified in Phase 07. Expected quality: this is not a channel that starts conversations, it is what makes channel 1 possible at 30+ cities. Its output is measured in qualified operators per city, target about 3. Honest note: a database of 5,000 hosts is worth nothing. The pipeline is judged only on how many quality touches it lets the founders make each week.

## 3. Referrals from Altoluxo

Joel has said publicly that he could not recommend us more. Ask for two named introductions once the case study is signed off. Expected quality: the highest of any channel, warm and peer-endorsed. Volume: tiny, two or three conversations total. Cost: one ask. Do it in September, before the soft launch.

## 4. Upwork outbound (intent-led)

Operators posting jobs for STR guest-comms VAs are buying exactly what Shonichi replaces, and they are buying now. Seb has a semi-optimised Upwork account already. Pitch: better, cheaper, round the clock. Expected quality: good intent, but posters skew small, so every one is screened against the 15-listing gate before a reply, and the reply must still be personal. Honest note: Upwork's terms restrict taking work off-platform, so the first exchange stays on Upwork and offers the pulse. Cost: time only. Status: account exists, optimise in Phase 04 alongside the templates.

## 5. Intent signals: job posts (priority flags, not a pipeline)

Guest-comms or STR VA vacancies on Indeed, LinkedIn Jobs, OnlineJobs.ph and Upwork. A hit on an operator already in Airtable raises its priority and gives the message its opening line. A hit on an unknown operator creates a lead for qualification. Expected quality: a flag, not a conversation. Cost: a weekly search, manual in v1.

## 7. Fiverr productised gig (inbound, scoped here, go/no-go at Phase 10)

Gig: "Airbnb listing audit and review intelligence report", about £95, which is the Property Pulse plus a 15-minute walkthrough call. Buyers self-identify as operators who pay for exactly what we give away in outreach; the call is the qualifier and the demo's first half. Expected quality: Fiverr buyers skew to single-unit hosts, so most will fail gate 1 or 2. Value is a trickle of pre-paid, pre-warmed conversations and a public proof point. Cost: an account (none yet), a gig page, delivery time. Decision: scope only, no build in September. The LinkedIn and pulse motion is the priority.

## 8. Reddit (content seeding, not outreach)

Hosts in r/airbnb_hosts, r/AirBnBHosts, r/ShortTermRentals and UK-specific threads ask the same operational questions repeatedly. Answer them properly under Seb's name, no pitching, no links unless asked. Feed FAQ-shaped content from what the reports and pulses surface. Expected quality: slow, indirect, untapped by competitors. Direct outreach on Reddit is a reputational risk and is not done. Cost: an hour a week at most.

## 9. Directories and watering holes (seeder lists for the pipeline)

STAA and ASAP member lists, Hostaway partner and customer pages, UK STR operator Facebook groups, Book Direct Show and Shortyz attendee and finalist lists, STR podcasts and their guests. Expected quality: these are sources of names for the pipeline, not conversation channels. The value is that a listed operator has already proven gate 1.
