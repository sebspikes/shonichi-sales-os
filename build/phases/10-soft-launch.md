## Context
The machine must touch reality before Seb's holiday (22 Sept - 3 Oct). Soft launch sends real messages to real operators from wave one, exposes what only reality exposes, and hands the live pipeline to Sotirios for the holiday window. First new customer by November is the standing goal; at 50 combined touches/week and rough cold-outreach rates (~100 quality touches → ~10 replies → ~3-4 demos → ~1 client), it is conservative.

## Objective
Both founders have sent real touches, the machine recorded all of it, the daily queue generates cleanly, and the Sardinia handover is written.

## Definition of done
≥10 real quality touches per founder sent and logged; replies (if any) handled through the state machine; friction from real sends fixed or queued; handover written; `build/QUEUE.md` status flipped to LIVE at the Sunday 20 Sept sign-off.

- **FN 18 Sept, launch review:** funnel snapshot (touches/replies so far), template performance first read, the v2 decision list reviewed WITH DATA:
